Privacy Policy

Effective October 26, 2026

Paper to PR turns your handwritten training journal into your training history. This policy explains what information the app handles, why, and the choices you have. It is written to match how the product actually works: your notebook is yours, your history is yours, and we do not sell personal data or use it for advertising.

What we collect

  • Account information. When you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive your name and email address (Apple may give us a private relay address instead of your real one, at your choice). We store this with your account so you can sign in on a new phone or the web and find your history.
  • Your workout library. The workouts, exercises, and sets you save, plus your benchmark settings (sex, height, reference bodyweight). Your library lives on your device and, when you are signed in, syncs to our server so it follows your account.
  • Content you import. Photos of journal pages and workout text you paste or upload, when you ask us to structure them (see "How imports are processed").
  • Subscription status. Purchases are handled by Apple. We and our subscription provider receive your subscription status (for example, trialing or active) — never your card details.
  • Waitlist email. If you join the launch waitlist on our website, we store your email address only to send you one email when the app launches.

What stays on your phone

Bodyweight from Apple Health is read on your device only. If you allow it, the app reads weight samples from HealthKit to scale your Strength Index per pound. Those samples never leave your phone, are never sent to our servers, and are never stored by us.

How imports are processed

When you import a journal photo or paste workout text, that content is sent over an encrypted connection to our server and processed by an AI model (provided by Anthropic) to structure it into a workout draft. When you import workouts — by photo or pasted text — the pages or text you submit are held on our server only while your import is being prepared and waiting for your device. They are deleted as soon as the finished import is delivered to your device, and always within 24 hours of submission. Import content is never used for anything else. Photo imports wait for your review before anything is saved to your library; text imports are added when they finish, and you can undo an import from its receipt.

How your information is used

  • To provide the app: your history, charts, PRs, and Strength Index, and sync across your devices.
  • To operate your account and subscription.
  • To respond when you contact us.

We do not sell personal information, share it for advertising, or use it to track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Service providers

We use a small number of providers strictly to run the service: Apple and Google for sign-in, Anthropic for AI structuring of imports, and Fly.io for hosting our backend and account database (United States). Each receives only what it needs to perform its function.

Your choices

  • Export. Your library is yours to take with you: you can export it at any time, including without an active subscription. Export is currently available directly in the app; how export is delivered may change over time, but your ability to get your data out will not be removed.
  • Delete your account. Deleting your account in the app permanently removes your account and your synced library from our servers. You choose whether the copy on your device is kept or wiped at the same time.
  • Leave the waitlist. Email hello@papertopr.com and we will remove your waitlist address.
  • Sign out. Signing out leaves your on-device library on your phone and stops sync until you sign in again.

Children

Paper to PR is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes materially, we will update this page and note the change in the app before it takes effect for existing accounts. Questions: hello@papertopr.com.
