How it works

The journal stays the original. The app does the bookkeeping.

01

Write it in ink

Train the way you already do. Your paper journal is the record — no typing sets into a phone between lifts, no app to feed mid-workout.

02

Snap the page

Photograph the page when you're done. Paper to PR reads your handwriting and drafts the session — you review every line before it's saved.

03

See the whole story

Every session joins your full history — trends, totals, and your Strength Index across months and years of training, not just this week.

Paper first

The notebook is the original and stays that way. Paper to PR reads your journal; it never replaces it.

Private by default

No feeds, no followers, no streaks to protect. Your training history is yours — built for the work, not an audience.

Honest numbers

Benchmarks anchored to your bodyweight and your lifts. Slow, real progress — measured in years, the way strength actually happens.
