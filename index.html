How it works

The journal stays the original. The app does the bookkeeping.

01

Write it in ink

Train the way you already do. Your paper journal is the record — no typing sets into a phone between lifts, no app to feed mid-workout.

02

Snap the page

Photograph the page when you're done. Paper to PR reads your handwriting and drafts the session — you review every line before it's saved. Nothing enters your history unedited.

03

See the whole story

Every session joins your full history — trends, totals, and your Strength Index across months and years of training, not just this week.

The Strength Index

One number for years of work.

The Index scores your squat, bench, and deadlift against benchmarks set from your own ideal bodyweight — not against anyone else. Watch it climb across a training career, one journal page at a time.

Import your back pages — years of them — and the Index reconstructs the story from the beginning.
